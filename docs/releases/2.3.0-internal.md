## Current product

- Playable, skippable first-run practice, replayable from Settings. Practice cannot grant records, currency or achievements. The first Hardcore run explains its one-hit rule.
- Offline Progress with **16 visible achievements** and completed-run statistics. The original twelve thresholds remain intact; First Contact, Finding Orbit, Hardcore Cadet and Steady Hands add earlier goals. Results identify newly earned achievements.
- One checked, versioned progress snapshot with legacy migration, coalesced writes, pending/error feedback and retry. Corrupt saves are preserved, including malformed legacy counters. Platform unlocks merge without revoking earned goals or inventing historical statistics.
- Settings with separate music/effects, master mute, optional haptics, language, practice, support/privacy/deletion links and native version/build. English and German cover the interface, semantics, help and native share text.
- Optional Android Play Games and iOS Game Center. Queue ownership stays bound to its original game-service profile; a different account cannot receive earlier pending updates. Offline play remains available. Returning to the original profile resumes sync.
- The active design follows the owner's later **shop-free** decision: Classic artwork, prominent Play/Try again and quiet Progress navigation. Shop, currency rewards and world selection are hidden; their code, assets, wallet IDs and saved balances/unlocks are preserved. New Horizons and World Tour remain defined and hidden until earned in platform catalogs. Honey Heist/Haunted Orbit are planned only if the collection is explicitly re-enabled.

The fixed 60 Hz simulation, original world/visible field, collision sizes, RNG and original twelve achievement rules remain covered by the released-Java oracle. Fixtures themselves are unchanged. The platform-event test maps legacy leaderboard IDs to their intended replacement IDs; it still checks original event conditions and ordering.
