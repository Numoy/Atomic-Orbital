# Atomic Orbital 2.3.0 (47) — Google Play

Published on 6 November 2026 to Google Play **Internal testing**, package `de.numpy.orbital.orbital4`. Play Console confirms **Available to internal testers** and an **Active** track.

This release commits the accumulated world-art and Play Games work and includes the changes made since Android build 46:

- World progression starts new players at zero Stars, with revised prices and achievement requirements for advanced worlds. Existing saved balances and owned worlds are preserved.
- Progress shows daily best duration, completed days, streaks, and all-time daily best, plus world records and collection progress.
- The hangar shows world tiers, unlock requirements, descriptions, ownership, and world records.
- Home, practice, Progress, and Settings receive visual polish with updated English/German text.
