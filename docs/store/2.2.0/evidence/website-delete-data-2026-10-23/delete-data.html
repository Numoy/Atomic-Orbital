Atomic Orbital — Delete Your Data

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Atomic Orbital has no developer-operated account or game-data server. You can remove local game records on your device and, on Android, delete the game's data held by Google Play Games separately. You do not need to delete your Google account.

1. Clear local data first

  1. Close Atomic Orbital and keep it closed until you finish the steps below.
  2. On Android, open Settings → Apps → Atomic Orbital → Storage & cache → Clear storage and confirm. Device manufacturers may use slightly different labels, such as “Clear data.”
  3. If you play on other devices, clear their Atomic Orbital data too before reconnecting them to Play Games.

This removes local personal bests, completed-game counts, total points, play time, preferences and pending or archived Play Games updates. Local progress cannot be recovered through the game. A system backup may restore old records later; restored records or another device's saved progress can be uploaded again when Play Games reconnects.

Clear local data before deleting Google-held game data. Disconnecting Play Games in the app only stops future uploads while disconnected. It does not delete local records, the pending upload queue or data already held by Google.

2. Delete Google Play Games data for Atomic Orbital

After clearing the local data, use the Google account connected to Play Games:

  1. Open your Play Games profile in a browser.
  2. Choose Your Data → Delete Play Games Account & Data.
  3. Under Delete individual game data, find Atomic Orbital and choose Delete.

You can also use the Play Store app: Profile icon → Settings → General → Play Games Profile → Privacy & Settings → Your Data → Delete profile & game data, then find Atomic Orbital under Delete individual game data and tap Delete. Follow Google's current deletion instructions if labels change.

This removes the game's Play Games records associated with that account, including game progress, achievements and leaderboard scores. Google's separate Play History is not deleted by this action; Google's instructions explain its own controls.

What is retained, and for how long?

Local game records remain until you clear or remove them; the developer does not retain a server copy. Google manages deletion and any retention of Google-held records under its privacy policy and retention policy, including any backup or legal/security retention. Atomic Orbital cannot set a separate deletion deadline for those records.

iPhone and iPad

Game records are local, with no Play Games or developer-operated cloud account. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading it keeps app data. A system backup may restore progress later.

Need help?

For deletion or privacy questions, contact Marvin März at marvin.maerz@gmail.com and identify Atomic Orbital and your platform. Do not send passwords or authentication codes. Support correspondence you choose to send is separate from the game's local and Google-held records.
