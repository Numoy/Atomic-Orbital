Atomic Orbital — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This notice covers Atomic Orbital for Android, iPhone and iPad. The developer and contact for this app is Marvin März: marvin.maerz@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your personal bests, completed-game count, total points, play time and sound preference locally. Android also stores your Play Games connection preference and pending score/achievement updates. An Android upgrade imports compatible settings from the original game. These records let the game remember your progress and choices. The developer does not operate a server that receives these local records.

Your operating system may include app data in its backup and restore features, depending on your device settings. The developer does not access those backups. Clearing app storage or removing the app can remove local progress; a system backup may restore it.

iPhone and iPad

The iOS version has no in-app account, online score service, developer analytics or advertising SDK. Gameplay and personal bests remain local. The app does not use Game Center or provide its own cross-device synchronization.

Android and Google Play Games

Android integrates Google Play Games for authentication, achievements and leaderboards. The SDK can initialize and attempt automatic authentication at launch when the app is connected. The app can read your Play Games player ID and display name, and sends eligible scores, completed-game counts and achievement updates to Google.

Google's SDK disclosure also describes gamer identity, analytics and diagnostics processing. Google uses HTTPS for this service and provides controls for deleting game data through a Play Games profile or Google Account. See Google's Play Games data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

You can play without signing in. Disconnecting Play Games in the app stops its future score/achievement uploads and retains pending updates locally. Reconnecting can send those pending updates. App disconnection does not remove your Android Google account, delete data already held by Google, or revoke the operating system's Play Games authentication. It is not a promise that an already-initialized SDK immediately stops all of its own processing.

Sharing a score

Tapping Share opens your device's system share menu with your score. You choose whether to continue and which app or recipient to use. The receiving service handles the information under its own privacy terms. The game does not upload this message to a developer-operated server.

Advertising and sensitive information

Atomic Orbital contains no advertising or in-app purchases. It does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and does not record audio. It does not collect health or financial information. The on-screen health gauge is only a game mechanic.

Retention and your choices

Local records stay on your device until removed, replaced or cleared. Pending Play Games updates are removed from the active queue after successful service acknowledgment; invalid or unreadable records may be retained locally for recovery. Google's retention and account controls apply to information held by Google; this app does not set a separate deletion period for Google's records.

For privacy questions or to exercise applicable data-protection rights, contact marvin.maerz@gmail.com. Please identify Atomic Orbital and your platform. Do not send account passwords or authentication codes.

Changes

We will update this notice when the app's data practices change. The date above identifies this version. This notice describes the app; visits to the developer's website are also subject to that website's privacy notice.
