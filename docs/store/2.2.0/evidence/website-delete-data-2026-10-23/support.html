Atomic Orbital — Support

Contact marvin.maerz@gmail.com for help with Atomic Orbital.

How to play

Hold the screen to move outward. Release to move inward. Collect the outlined energy pickups and avoid incoming photons. Each pickup reverses your direction. New orbits appear as your score increases.

In Normal mode, health regenerates. In Hardcore, one hit ends your run. Each mode has its own personal best. The same playfield and rules apply on phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape.

Sound

On a fresh install, sound starts muted. Use the sound button in the menu or results screen to enable or mute music and effects. If sound is enabled but inaudible, check the device's media volume and selected audio output. On iPhone/iPad, also check the device's sound settings.

Scores and Play Games

Personal bests are saved on your device. Removing the app or clearing its storage can remove them. System backup and restore behavior depends on device settings.

On Android, Google Play Games adds achievements and online leaderboards. An internet connection and a Play Games account are needed for those features. You can play offline or without signing in. Pending service updates are saved locally and retried when connected. Disconnecting in the app stops future uploads; it does not delete your Google account or existing Google-held game data.

The iPhone/iPad version uses local personal bests and does not include Game Center, online leaderboards or app-provided cross-device synchronization.

Report a problem

Please include the app version, device model, operating-system version, selected game mode and the steps needed to reproduce the problem. A screenshot can help. Send only information you are comfortable sharing; never send passwords, payment details or authentication codes.
