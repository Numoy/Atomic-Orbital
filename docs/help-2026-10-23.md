# How to play — 2026-10-23

The menu and pause screen now share a How to play sheet. It explains held/released radial movement, keyboard controls, square energy pickups and their direction reversal, red photons, Normal/Hardcore rules, and pausing. The sheet reuses the existing palette, utility controls and play-button style. It adds no assets, dependencies, settings or mandatory onboarding.

Help does not start a run. Opening it from pause keeps the existing run frozen, and Got It, Android Back or Escape return to the pause screen. Resume remains a separate action. The simulation, collision rules, scores and visible playfield are unchanged.

The sheet scrolls at large text sizes and in short windows, respects safe areas, exposes a heading to assistive technology, and excludes underlying menu controls from the active semantics tree.
