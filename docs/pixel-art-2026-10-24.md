# Pixel-art game and world shop — 2026-10-24

Historical record: the user subsequently chose a smoother style that fits the game. The [orbital style follow-up](orbital-style-2026-10-24.md) supersedes the pixel artwork and font in the runtime; the source artwork and evidence remain as design history.

Implements the user's selected retro pixel-art direction in the actual Flutter game, menus, results and shop. The earlier illustrations in `docs/design/` remain concept references; the screenshots below render the production widgets with bundled artwork.

## What changed

- Cow Abduction now uses a shaded pixel Earth, metallic UFO and full-body cow sprite. Deep Blue uses an ocean world, yellow submarine and pixel pearl.
- Themed gameplay uses a quiet star field, crisp orbit guides, pixel HUD, health meter and pause control. Scores sit above the arena, leaving the world artwork unobscured. Classic retains its existing gameplay renderer and native Android glyphs.
- The shop features one world at a time, with a large scene preview, compact balance, gold unlock control and three world thumbnails. The cow preview includes a pixel tractor beam; the underwater preview has bubbles.
- Browsing with thumbnails or left/right keys only changes the preview. Unlocking or choosing an owned world equips it explicitly. Back remains accessible while scrolling; the shop supports screen-reader actions and large text.
- Pixel headings and controls carry through the menu, pause and results screens. Paragraphs use Roboto for readability. Themed result scores sit outside the planet.

Five PNG sprites are bundled in `assets/pixel/`. They were generated with the built-in image-generation tool; their [exact prompts and source record](design/pixel-implementation-2026-10-24/generation.json) are saved. The source PNGs are unchanged. Runtime loading trims transparent padding, decodes cores at 64 pixels and actors at 32 pixels, and paints with nearest-neighbor filtering. The Press Start 2P bitmap-style font is bundled unmodified with its SIL OFL and FONTLOG notices; its pinned official Google Fonts source is recorded alongside the prompts.

## Preserved behavior and recovery

No gameplay-controller, collision, timing, random-number, score-submission or wallet logic changed in this pass. World IDs, prices, balances and owned selections use the existing save format. The 100-Stardust welcome balance, one Stardust per pickup and 150-Stardust world prices remain in effect. Deep Blue's artwork changed from the initial pufferfish prototype to the approved ocean-world direction.

The sprite loader shares concurrent requests and publishes only a complete set. It disposes partial decodes after failure and supports retry. A selected themed run cannot advance or accept input before its artwork is ready. A load error offers Retry, Use Classic and Return to menu; recovery controls scroll on short screens with large text. Classic can run without themed artwork. Buying or equipping still requires a successful wallet save.
